# Awesome-Medical-Device-Quality-Management

## Top Medical Device Quality Management Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on eQMS, Design Controls, CAPA, Risk Management, Document Control, ISO 13485 & FDA QMSR Compliance*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Medical Device Quality Management Systems (eQMS)**. These systems help medtech companies manage design controls, risk files, controlled documents, CAPA, change control, training, audits, and post-market processes in alignment with ISO 13485, FDA QMSR (21 CFR Part 820), and related regulations.



**Examples** include Greenlight Guru, MasterControl, Qualio, QT9 QMS, Arena QMS, Sparta TrackWise, Veeva QualityOne, ETQ Reliance, ComplianceQuest, and Qualtrax (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Fully validated, audit-ready eQMS platforms for medical devices are almost exclusively commercial. Open options (Git-based QMS scaffolds, Odoo/ERPNext quality modules, document-control tools) can support documentation discipline but generally lack the validation packages, electronic signatures, and regulatory pedigree required for FDA/ISO-regulated use. This section is realistic about those limits.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Greenlight Guru](https://www.greenlight.guru/)**  

  Medtech-specific eQMS built for design controls, risk management (ISO 14971), and alignment with ISO 13485 and FDA QMSR—widely used by medical device companies.



- **[MasterControl](https://www.mastercontrol.com/)**  

  Enterprise quality and manufacturing platform extensively adopted in life sciences and medical devices for document control, CAPA, training, and compliance.



- **[Qualio](https://www.qualio.com/)**  

  Modern cloud eQMS designed for life-science and medtech teams, covering document control, training, CAPA, and design-control workflows.



- **[QT9 QMS](https://qt9software.com/)**  

  Cloud quality management system offering document control, CAPA, audits, training, and related modules used by manufacturers including medical device firms.



- **[Arena QMS (PTC)](https://www.ptc.com/)**  

  Quality management capabilities integrated with product lifecycle processes, supporting regulated product development including medical devices.



- **[Sparta TrackWise (Honeywell)](https://www.honeywell.com/)**  

  Established enterprise QMS used in highly regulated industries for deviations, CAPA, change control, complaints, and audit management.



- **[Veeva QualityOne](https://www.veeva.com/)**  

  Cloud quality solution within the Veeva Vault ecosystem, popular in life sciences for controlled documents and quality processes.



- **[ETQ Reliance](https://www.etq.com/)**  

  Flexible QMS platform covering document control, CAPA, change management, audits, and risk for regulated manufacturers including medtech.



- **[ComplianceQuest](https://www.compliancequest.com/)**  

  Cloud QHSE and quality management platform built on Salesforce, used for document control, CAPA, audits, and compliance in regulated sectors.



- **[Qualtrax](https://www.qualtrax.com/)**  

  Compliance and quality management software focused on document control, audits, CAPA, and training for regulated organizations.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Open QMS / GitHub-native QMS frameworks](https://github.com/)**  

  Emerging open-source approaches that model document approval, CAPA, change control, and training as version-controlled, reviewable workflows (useful for process discipline, not as a validated eQMS).



- **[Odoo Quality modules](https://github.com/odoo/odoo)**  

  Open-source quality management apps that can support inspections and basic nonconformance tracking; require significant extension and validation for medical-device use.



- **[ERPNext Quality features](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)**  

  Open quality inspection and procedure capabilities within ERPNext—suitable as a starting point only after rigorous validation and procedural controls.



- **[Document control and wiki/Git-based QMS scaffolds](https://github.com/)**  

  Open templates and tools that implement controlled-document lifecycles using Git, wikis, or simple databases (common in early-stage or non-device contexts).



- **[CAPA and nonconformance open trackers](https://github.com/)**  

  Lightweight open issue-tracking configurations specialized for CAPA and deviation workflows.



- **[Risk management and FMEA open toolkits](https://github.com/)**  

  Open templates and scripts for ISO 14971-style risk analysis and FMEA that can feed into a formal QMS.



- **[Training record open systems](https://github.com/)**  

  Simple open tools for assigning and recording training on controlled procedures.



- **[Design-control and requirements open tools](https://github.com/)**  

  Community tools (e.g., Doorstop-style requirements management) that can support aspects of design history files when properly controlled.



- **[Audit and supplier-quality open prototypes](https://github.com/)**  

  Experimental open applications for audit planning, findings, and supplier quality tracking.



- **[ISO 13485 / procedure template repositories](https://github.com/)**  

  Open collections of procedure and work-instruction templates that organizations adapt under their own document control.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Using **Git-based or Open QMS scaffolds** to enforce review and approval discipline for procedures and records.

- Leveraging **Odoo or ERPNext** quality modules only as part of a broader, validated system—not as a standalone medical-device eQMS.

- Combining open risk and requirements tools with a commercial eQMS for hybrid workflows.

- Accepting that validated electronic signatures, Part 11/Annex 11 compliance, audit-ready validation packages, and medtech-specific best practices still require commercial platforms (Greenlight Guru, MasterControl, Qualio, Veeva, TrackWise, ETQ, etc.).

- Focusing open-source efforts on transparency of procedures, early-stage documentation, and non-regulated supporting processes.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Maintain controlled documents and design history in a commercial eQMS → optionally use open tools for early drafting, risk analysis, or internal collaboration → ensure all GxP records remain under validated control. Suitable only under qualified quality and regulatory oversight. Most medical-device companies standardize on commercial eQMS platforms for compliance and inspection readiness.

## Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Medical device quality systems are subject to strict regulatory requirements (FDA QMSR, ISO 13485, EU MDR, etc.). Open-source or self-built solutions are generally **not** suitable as the primary eQMS for regulated medical devices without extensive validation, procedural controls, and regulatory review. Use of non-validated systems for GxP records can create significant compliance risk. This list is not regulatory, legal, or compliance advice.
